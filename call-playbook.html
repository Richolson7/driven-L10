1. Pitch base (memorize)

We engineer the cases, film and skids, keep safety stock on hand, and deliver on our own truck — so customers have one number to call.

2. Opener (memorize)

"Hi, this is [name] with Driven Solutions in River Falls. I'll be quick. We supply the cases, stretch wrap, skids and film plants ship on. We keep backup stock for you and deliver on our own truck. Who handles packaging buying there?"

3. When you reach the buyer

"I'm not calling to sell you anything today. I just want to see if it's worth 15 minutes with Rich, our owner."

4. Four discovery questions (memorize, ask in order)

  1. "What do you ship your product in? Cases, pallets, stretch wrap?"
  2. "Who supplies that now, and how's it going?"
  3. "Have you ever run short or had a late delivery hold up a line?"
  4. "Anything changing soon, like new lines, more volume or a new building?"

5. Objection handling (memorize)

Objection handling
#ObjectionResponse (word for word)Follow-up action
1 Not interested "Totally fair. Is that because you're set on packaging, or just bad timing?" Then ask discovery question 4.
2 We already have a provider "Most people we talk to do. We're usually the backup that turns into the main supplier when somebody runs short. Would it hurt to have a second option?" none stated
3 Just send me info "Happy to. So I send the right thing, what do you buy the most of?" Then: "Rich can walk you through it in 15 minutes. Is Tuesday or Thursday better?" Choice close for a 15-minute meeting with Rich.
4 Call me back later "Sure. What day and time works best?" Calendar it on the call; call back exactly then.

6. Qualifying checklist: book only if every box is checked

  • They ship physical product
  • They buy cases, wrap, skids or film regularly
  • You're talking to the buyer, or someone who will get Rich in front of the buyer
  • They're within reach of our truck (Rich to confirm distance rule for setters)
  • They've mentioned at least one problem or upcoming change

7. Close / hand-off: booking the meeting (memorize)

"Sounds like it's worth a quick sit-down. Rich, our owner, handles these himself. Does Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2 work for 15 minutes?"

8. After the call: log it (from "CRM logging rules")

  • Log every call and every booked meeting the same day
  • Every new lead/prospect is owned by Rich unless assigned
  • Check suppression / dedupe before adding records
  • Visits/calls should include a clear NEXT_STEP (e.g. NEXT_STEP: follow_up YYYY-MM-DD or meeting time) plus notes
  • Do not invent contacts, emails, or results
  • Setter-specific CRM fields / screens: Rich to confirm

9. Words to use and avoid on calls

From the source glossary: "Use plain language on calls. Don't oversell engineering — Rich handles that."

In-house delivery:
Driven's own truck to the customer, not a brokered carrier move.
Safety stock:
backup inventory held so the line doesn't stop.
VMI:
"we keep backup stock and manage the program," not brokerage.
  • Inbound freight (carriers bringing material in to Driven) is not a service Driven sells.
