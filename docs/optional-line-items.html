Optional line items on quotes

A quote can carry lines the buyer may take or leave. The quote stays as one document. You do not rebuild it when they choose.

For the rep

  1. Open the quote. On a line the buyer can skip, tick Optional. Included starts unchecked, so that line is out of the total.
  2. Tick Included when you want it in the price before you send the quote, or after the buyer says they want it.
  3. The quote total, margin, freight that sits on a line, and the deal value count required lines and included optional lines.
  4. The totals area shows the rest as Optional add-ons: $X not included.
  5. Print and Download PDF list optional lines and their prices. Included optional lines stay in the quote total and are marked Optional. The others sit under Optional add-ons.
  6. When the deal is won, only included lines copy onto the order. Optional lines the buyer left off stay on the quote.

For the buyer

The printed quote and the PDF show each optional line marked Optional, with its price. Optional add-ons that are not included are listed under the total and are not part of that total. The buyer tells Driven which ones they want. There is no separate page where the buyer ticks the lines.

The rep ticks Included for the lines the buyer chose. The total updates, and the deal history records Customer selected options.

Where it is stored

Optional and Included are saved on each line in the quote on the deal. They do not need a new database column. The order keeps the included lines. The quote keeps every line, including the optional ones that were left off.
